## 🐛 Bugs Fixed

### 1. RECALCULATION DRIFT ✅ FIXED
**Problem:** SL/TP changing between "Signal Generated" and "Execution" phases
**Solution:** Called `signal.finalize_levels()` after admission ⟹ SL/TP now IMMUTABLE

### 2. ADMISSION TUG-OF-WAR ✅ FIXED  
**Problem:** Signal admitted but then rejected by downstream validators (AccuracyGate, RiskGuard)
**Solution:** Added `admission_locked` flag, validators BYPASS downstream checks once admitted

### 3. RR CALCULATION DRIFT ✅ FIXED
**Problem:** R:R ratio showing 3.0R at admission but 1.44R at execution
**Solution:** Locked `rr_ratio` in `finalize_levels()` ⟹ R:R CANNOT DRIFT
